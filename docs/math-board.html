Week 2 · Stage 1 · g-limit

Shrinking a too-big acceleration command

Symbols

a_cmd
acceleration PN asks for (m/s²), a vector
a_max
largest acceleration the cat can pull (m/s²), a number
|a_cmd|
length of a_cmd

Pieces

  1. Clip each part on its own (lands on the box):
    \[ a_i = \min\big(\max(a_{\text{cmd},i},\,-a_{\max}),\; a_{\max}\big) \quad i = x, y, z \]
  2. Scale the whole vector (lands on the circle), only when \( |a_{\text{cmd}}| > a_{\max} \):
    \[ a = a_{\text{cmd}} \cdot \frac{a_{\max}}{|a_{\text{cmd}}|} \]
  3. Length of a vector:
    \[ |a| = \sqrt{a_x^2 + a_y^2 + a_z^2} \]

Result

Clip → (100, 0, 100), z/x = 1 (flatter). Scale → (60, 0, 80), z/x = 1.33 (same direction as PN). Scale wins.

Turn radius

At constant speed \( v \), sideways acceleration \( a \) bends the path into a circle:

\[ r = \frac{v^2}{a} \]

Capping \( a \) at \( a_{\max} \) sets the tightest turn \( r_{\min} = v^2 / a_{\max} \). 1 g ≈ 9.81 m/s².

Chosen: \( v = 60 \) m/s, \( a_{\max} = 100 \) m/s² \( \Rightarrow r_{\min} = 36 \) m.

PN inputs

\[ r = p_{\text{mouse}} - p_{\text{cat}} \qquad v_{\text{rel}} = v_{\text{mouse}} - v_{\text{cat}} \]
\[ V_c = -\frac{r \cdot v_{\text{rel}}}{|r|} \qquad \Omega = \frac{r \times v_{\text{rel}}}{r \cdot r} \]

\( V_c > 0 \): closing. \( \Omega = 0 \): line of sight not swinging (collision course).

Head-on check: \( r = (100,0,0),\ v_{\text{rel}} = (-60,0,0) \Rightarrow V_c = 60,\ \Omega = 0 \) (no turn needed).

PN command

\[ a = N \, V_c \, (\Omega \times \hat r) \qquad \hat r = \frac{r}{|r|} \]

\( N \): unitless gain, textbook range 3–5. Then scale to \( a_{\max} \) if \( |a| > a_{\max} \).

Your step: pick \( N \) and say why.
