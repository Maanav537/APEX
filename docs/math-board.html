Week 2 · Stage 2 · sensor noise

Smoothing a noisy v_rel

Symbols

z_k
raw r from the sensor at tick k (m), noisy
dt
0.01 s (100 Hz controller)
σ
noise spread on each r reading (m)
v̂, r̂
filter's estimate (hat = estimate, not truth)

The problem

\[ v_{\text{meas}} = \frac{z_k - z_{k-1}}{dt} \qquad \sigma_v \approx \frac{\sqrt{2}\,\sigma}{dt} = \frac{\sqrt 2 \cdot 1}{0.01} \approx 141 \text{ m/s} \]

Three filter types

  1. Moving average: average the last \( M \) raw values. Lag ≈ \( \tfrac{M-1}{2}\,dt \).
    \[ \hat v_k = \frac{1}{M}\sum_{j=0}^{M-1} v_{\text{meas},\,k-j} \]
  2. Exponential (low-pass): nudge the estimate toward each new value by a fraction \( \alpha \in (0,1] \). Remembers one number.
    \[ \hat v_k = \hat v_{k-1} + \alpha\,(v_{\text{meas}} - \hat v_{k-1}) \]
  3. Alpha-beta tracker: predict where r should be, compare to the reading, correct both r̂ and v̂. Never differences raw readings.
    \[ r_{\text{pred}} = \hat r_{k-1} + \hat v_{k-1}\,dt \qquad e = z_k - r_{\text{pred}} \]
    \[ \hat r_k = r_{\text{pred}} + \alpha\,e \qquad \hat v_k = \hat v_{k-1} + \frac{\beta}{dt}\,e \]

Chosen: alpha-beta

Start: \( \hat r_0 = z_0,\ \hat v_0 = 0 \). Remembers \( \hat r, \hat v \).

\( \alpha \to 1 \): trust the reading (noisy, fast). \( \alpha \to 0 \): trust the prediction (smooth, slow). Common pairing:

\[ \beta = \frac{\alpha^2}{2 - \alpha} \]

Settling takes roughly \( 1/\alpha \) ticks for \( \hat r \), longer for \( \hat v \).

Your step: pick \( \sigma \) (m) and \( \alpha \), then solve for \( \beta \) with the pairing formula.
