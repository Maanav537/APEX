Week 2 · Stage 3 · Linear Kalman Filter (Solved)

Complete 3-State Kalman Filter Loop

1 · Predict (push state & uncertainty forward by dt)

\[ p_{\text{pred}} = \hat p + \hat v\,dt + \tfrac{1}{2}\hat a\,dt^2, \qquad v_{\text{pred}} = \hat v + \hat a\,dt, \qquad a_{\text{pred}} = \hat a \]
\[ P_{\text{pred}} = F P F^T + Q, \qquad F = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & dt & \tfrac{1}{2}dt^2 \\ 0 & 1 & dt \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \]

2 · Distance-scaled measurement variance R(d)

\[ R(d) = \sigma_r^2 + (d \cdot \sigma_\theta)^2 \]

At \( d = 2000\text{ m} \): \( R(2000) = 1^2 + (2000 \cdot 0.001)^2 = 1 + 4 = 5\text{ m}^2 \).
At \( d = 50\text{ m} \): \( R(50) = 1^2 + (50 \cdot 0.001)^2 = 1 + 0.0025 = 1.0025\text{ m}^2 \).

3 · Solved Innovation Variance S and Gains K

\[ S = P_{\text{pred}}(0,0) + R(d) \]
\[ K_0 = \frac{P_{\text{pred}}(0,0)}{S}, \qquad K_1 = \frac{P_{\text{pred}}(1,0)}{S}, \qquad K_2 = \frac{P_{\text{pred}}(2,0)}{S} \]

4 · Correct (nudge estimates and shrink uncertainty)

\[ e = z - p_{\text{pred}}, \qquad \hat p = p_{\text{pred}} + K_0\,e, \qquad \hat v = v_{\text{pred}} + K_1\,e, \qquad \hat a = a_{\text{pred}} + K_2\,e \]
\[ P = (I - K H)\,P_{\text{pred}} \]
